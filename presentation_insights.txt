TRAFFIC DATASET — PRESENTATION INSIGHTS

Dataset used for main analysis:
• Augmented dataset: 2,599 observations, 9 variables.
• No missing values detected.
• No exact duplicate rows detected.

Key findings:
1. Traffic-level distribution is dominated by Medium traffic (62.2%).
2. High traffic accounts for 25.0%.
3. Very High traffic is rare in the augmented data (1.0%).
4. Average vehicle activity peaks around hour 9:00 at 43.0 vehicles.
5. Highway has the highest average vehicle count among area types (51.0).
6. Market has the highest average pedestrian count (24.1).
7. Vehicle and pedestrian counts show a moderate positive correlation (0.50).
8. Ambient light follows a strong day/night cycle, peaking around midday.
9. Weekday and weekend average vehicle counts are very similar in this dataset (36.1 vs 36.0).
10. The original and augmented traffic-level distributions differ substantially: original Very High = 18.0%, augmented Very High = 1.0%.

Important interpretation note:
The augmented dataset should be treated as a synthetic/expanded dataset rather than as a direct representation of the original 100-row distribution. The comparison chart makes this distribution shift explicit.
